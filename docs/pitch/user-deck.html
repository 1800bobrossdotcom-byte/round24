01 — The problemSound familiar?

Rent is tracked to the dollar. Labor lives in pay logs, texts, and memory.

Labor is the biggest controllable cost in your buildings — and the one you can't see per door. When an owner asks what a unit actually cost to maintain, the honest answer is a guess. Round24 measures it.

The Friday spreadsheet

  • ×The pay log, the rent roll, and the P&L are three different files — none of them agree.
  • ×A tech's day gets split across four buildings from memory, a week later.
  • ×Receipts in a glovebox, hours on a text thread, nothing tied to a door.
  • ×"What does that building cost us to run?" — nobody can answer it with a straight face.

With Round24

  • →One ledger. Timers, receipts, leases, and building expenses in the same system.
  • →Hours land on the building as they happen — the crew allocates from the door, not the office on Friday.
  • →A per-building P&L that updates the moment a timer closes or a receipt is approved.
  • →Crew on phones, office on desktop, residents at the door — everyone on one system.

08 — Built to be trustedPay data is sensitive data

Above-market security, because it holds your payroll.

Pay rates, operator identities, and financials are encrypted at rest with keys that belong to your organization. Every read and write is scoped to your org and your role — enforced at the database, not the app.

Field-level encryption

AES-256-GCM + AWS KMS

Pay and financial fields are encrypted per org with envelope keys. Fail-closed: if a key can't be reached, nothing is shown.

Row-level security

Deny by default

Every query is scoped to the organization and role at the database. There is no cross-tenant path to write.

Role separation

Crew see the field

Contractors get field tools only; financials and the P&L are gated to the office. Invites are role-scoped from day one.

Compliance

On the record

Credential expiry, contractor paperwork, and an audit trail of sensitive actions — approvals, invites, exports, renames.

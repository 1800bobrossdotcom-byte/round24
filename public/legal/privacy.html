This Privacy Policy explains how Caliper, operated by Evolution24 Property Management (“Caliper”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with the Caliper platform at caliper.solutions and related applications and services (the “Service”). It works alongside our Terms of Service.

Controller vs. processor. When your employer or organization (the “Customer”) uses Caliper to manage its operations, the Customer is the controller of the workspace data and Caliper acts as its processor — we handle that data on the Customer’s instructions. For your individual account, security, and communications with us, Caliper is the controller. If you are an employee or contractor of a Customer, direct data-rights requests about workspace data to your workspace Admin first.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account & profile — email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), display name, phone number, role, and workspace membership.
  • Compliance & payroll information you choose to add — emergency contact details; contractor tax details for year-end 1099s (legal name, business name, tax classification, mailing address, and only the last four digits of a TIN/SSN — never the full number); and licenses, insurance, or certifications with their expiry dates.
  • Operational content — work orders, time entries, availability status, messages and notes, purchase records, and receipt or document images you upload.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & device data — log data such as actions taken, timestamps, IP address, browser and device type, and error diagnostics.
  • Audit records — for security and compliance, we record certain sensitive actions (for example, purchase approvals, invitations, data exports, and workspace renames) together with the user who performed them.
  • Local storage — we use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences (see Section 12).

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, secure, and maintain the Service and your workspace;
  • To authenticate users, enforce role-based access, and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • To power features you request — including receipt reading/itemization, price-matching, scheduling, and reporting;
  • To maintain audit and compliance records on behalf of the Customer;
  • To provide support, respond to requests, and send service-related communications;
  • To diagnose problems, analyze usage in aggregate, and improve the Service; and
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Customer Data for advertising.

3. AI processing

Certain features send content to a trusted AI provider (currently Anthropic) to generate output — for example, a receipt image is processed to extract line items, vendor, totals, and the last four digits of a payment card, and messages may be summarized on request. This content is processed only to return a result to you. It is not used to train third-party AI models. AI output can be imperfect and is provided to assist human judgment, not to make automated decisions about you without human review.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we (or the Customer as controller) rely on: performance of a contract (to provide the Service); legitimate interests (to secure and improve the Service and prevent abuse); consent (where you choose to add optional information such as tax or emergency-contact details); and compliance with legal obligations.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed:

  • Within your workspace — according to role. For example, workspace Admins and Managers can see compliance and paperwork status for their team; contractor tax details are restricted to authorized office staff.
  • With sub-processors — service providers that host and operate the Service on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection obligations (Section 6).
  • For legal reasons — to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Caliper, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — if Caliper is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred subject to this Policy.

7. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic is served over TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest — sensitive identity fields — including a contractor’s legal name, tax identifiers, mailing address, and emergency-contact details — are additionally encrypted at the application layer with AES-256-GCM, using per-workspace envelope encryption backed by AWS KMS. All data is also protected by encryption at rest provided by our infrastructure providers.
  • Tenant isolation — every workspace is isolated with database row-level security, so one workspace cannot access another’s data.
  • Access controls — role-based permissions, optional two-factor authentication, and an append-only audit log of sensitive actions.
  • Data minimization — for example, we store only the last four digits of a TIN/SSN, never the full number.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards and to promptly address vulnerabilities.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account or workspace is active and as needed to provide the Service. When a workspace or account is closed, we delete or de-identify Customer Data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Backups are cycled out on a rolling basis. Audit-log records are retained to support security and compliance.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Caliper provides self-service tools for the most common requests directly in the app’s Settings screen:

  • Access & portability — “Download my data” exports the personal information we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Correction — you can update your profile, contact, and compliance details at any time.
  • Deletion — “Request account deletion” submits an erasure request for an Admin to process.

Because workspace data is controlled by your Customer, we may direct certain requests to your workspace Admin, or act on the Customer’s instructions. To exercise rights that aren’t covered by the in-app tools, contact us at privacy@caliper.solutions. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights, and you may appeal a decision by replying to our response.

10. Children

The Service is intended for use by businesses and their adult staff and contractors. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Data location & transfers

The Service is operated from and its data is primarily stored in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., you understand that your information will be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our sub-processors operate, which may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for international transfers.

12. Cookies & local storage

Caliper uses your browser’s local storage and strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, remember your portal and display preferences, and operate core features. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear this data through your browser, but doing so will sign you out and reset preferences.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the Service or by email and update the “Last updated” date above. Your continued use of the Service after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

14. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Policy or your personal information? Contact us at privacy@caliper.solutions.
